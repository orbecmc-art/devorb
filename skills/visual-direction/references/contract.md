# The contract

`## Direção visual` in the spec. Short lines, no values. Each field starts with its label exactly as written here, whatever the spec's language; what follows the label is in the spec's language. `devorb:design-system`, `devorb:motion`, the builder and `devorb:ui-review` read these labels. Fields, in this order:

- `Mode`: Persuade, Operate, Read or Experience. `Invention level`: Extend, New surface, same world, New world or Replicate.
- `Motion level: Suave | Equilibrado | Impacto`: keep the one that fits; on Replicate, the reference's.
- `Reference pack: design/referencias/<slug>/ (até 3)`: the pack this page follows (Replicate, or any page built from a captured reference); omit when there is none.
- `Same as reference:` and `Changed:` (Replicate): what stays as in the reference and what follows the project, from step 3 of `references/replicate.md`.
- `Sources:` consulted, skipped (why), the foundation, the specialist per job, the standout component (one at most, none on Operate) and new libraries to install.
- `Direction:` the world it comes from, the one idea, what the usual page in this category does that this one won't, and the mirror-test change. On Replicate, the reference is the direction: name it.
- `Type:` families and roles, confirmed or pending.
- `First screen:` what sits where, and the primary action.
- `Sections:` one item per section or region, in order, from the visitor's job: its pattern, where its text comes from, and its movement. The last item says how the page ends.
- `Material:` given, to show, missing; where each gap shows.
- `Copy:` the primary action's exact wording and the voice in one line.
- `Platform:` (app screens only) each platform's own idiom (Apple's guidelines on iOS, Material 3 on Android), or the neutral treatment chosen and why.
- `States:` for every feature that loads data, submits or depends on access: what the user sees when loading, empty, failed, succeeded, partly loaded and not allowed, what triggers it and the next action. A state that does not apply says why.
- `Floor:` `references/floor.md` holds; list any reflex broken, with this page's reason.
- `Do not:` what this page must not do: the reflexes and spent looks it avoids, and on Replicate the reference's text, photos and logo, plus anything under `Não levar`.
