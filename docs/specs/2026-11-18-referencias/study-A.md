## Synthesis

**Numbers**
- **H1 at 1440:** 46–140px, median ~64–72 (SaaS 54–96, restaurant 140). Tracking -0.01 to -0.05em (-0.04/-0.05 at 72px+); caps display +0.04em. Weights 300–600; only lead-gen uses 900.
- **Body:** 13–18px, median 14–15 (devtools 13–14, restaurant/fintech 16–18).
- **Ratio (H1/body):** 2.9–6.9, typically 4.2–5.5.
- **Sections per page:** 4–13, usually 8–11.
- **Spacing:** 4px grid; section padding 96–160px (restaurant 144–270); card gaps 16–24.
- **Radii:** 0 (luxury), 4–8 (devtools, buttons), 12–16 (cards), 24 (section sheets), 99 (pills).
- **Accents:** exactly 1 on 9 of 10 sites (Hoy: none), plus a grey for two-tone text.

**Hero patterns**
1. **Centred + product shot below:** pill eyebrow, H1 72–96 on 2 lines, sub 18–20, 1–2 pill CTAs, micro-caption, then a full-width UI frame at radius 12–16 on a soft glow (aave, billow, futureagi).
2. **Split header + bleeding UI:** H1 left, sub + CTA right, UI cropped at the right viewport edge (interfere).
3. **Left H1 + video canvas:** left text, CTA + "New:" link on one row, full-width product video (framer).
4. **Text left / device right over a blueprint grid:** dashed construction lines behind a phone mock (accessgrid).
5. **Copy + form card:** dark photo collage, gradient keyword in the H1, 2-step form at right, proof ticker underneath (agenciadevalor).
6. **Full-bleed photo + script-over-caps:** darkened photo, display 140 caps, script overlay, micro-caps tagline (bordo).
7. **Tilted content wall:** perspective grid of real content tiles fading behind the H1 (aifont).
8. **Floating avatar constellation:** blurred circles around a centred two-tone H1 (hoy).

**Section patterns**
- **Bento of live UI tiles:** 6–9 tiles of mixed span, each holding real data, not icons.
- **Proof grid:** dotted 4x3 cells mixing logos, stat numbers and short quotes.
- **Stat cards:** 3x2 grid, each with a mono label + status dot, a 48–64px number with a small unit, and a caption.
- **Alternating feature rows:** H2 + body, then a UI screenshot fading at the bottom.
- **Numbered steps:** 3–5 steps as accordion + sticky visual, or as a T-minus timeline.
- **Two-tone statement:** a 36px paragraph with highlighted keywords.
- **Integrations:** logo grid with filters and counts, or a hub diagram.
- **FAQ:** 4–8 rows, hairline-separated, "+" icon, one column at ~600px.
- **Changelog / "latest":** list with date micro-labels.
- **Dark band:** one inverted section on light sites for rhythm.

**What separates these from AI-generated pages**
- Real UI or real photos, never icons or stock.
- One accent plus two-tone grey headlines; tight negative display tracking.
- Hairlines (1px, 6–10% alpha), visible grid/rail lines, mono micro-labels, status dots.
- UI bleeding off the edge or fading at the bottom.
- Asymmetric section headers (H2 left, small button right).
- Pills only on buttons and chips; 96–160px spacing with density *inside* cards.
- One sustained metaphor (mission control, blueprint, ornamented menu).
- Specific numbers ("$27.7B", "<100ms", "+3.200").
- Mobile recomposed, not just stacked: visual dropped, hero left-aligned, CTAs full-width.
