## Synthesis

**Numbers.** Headline 47–80px for SaaS (median about 62); 180–200px for portfolio and editorial words. Mobile h1 32–64. Body 13–16 (median 15; four sites at 14 or below). Lead 18–24. Ratio 3.1–5.0 (onlines about 11). Tracking grows with size: -0.04 to -0.06em at 60–75px. Display weight is mostly **400–500**; only 3 of 11 sites go 600+. Families: 1–3. Grotesk + mono labels on 6 of 11; 3 add a serif or slab with italic accent words. Sections: 3–14, typically 8–10. Spacing scale 64/80/128/160/200/256. Radii: 9999 pill CTAs (7 of 11), cards 6–8 for dev/enterprise and 16–24 for consumer. Accents: 0–1 per site. 5 sites alternate dark and light, mostly as single contrast bands.

**Hero patterns.**
1. *Inline-object headline*: icons between the words of a 72–80px centered 3-line h1.
2. *Two-tone statement*: gray line + black line at the same size.
3. *Type-as-demo*: the headline performs the product (blur, avatar dot-matrix, selection handles).
4. *Copy left + real UI right + halo*: 5/7 columns, 600–800px blurred glow behind the UI only.
5. *One object cropped by the fold*: render or cut-out photo, nothing else.
6. *Giant word on an artifact canvas*: 180px word, 40px grid, toasts and cursors.
7. *Light serif on a hazy photo*: 54px serif, bottom-left, with a floating chat transcript.

**Section patterns.**
1. *Mono label + h2 split*: 12px "02 · LABEL", h2 42–56px left, 16px paragraph right.
2. *Readout bento*: 5–7 tiles, r 12–16, 1px border at 8% white, one big number + one chip each.
3. *Mono spec table*: uppercase keys, right-aligned values, hairline rows.
4. *Numbered before→after story* with italic captions.
5. *Big-number band*: 3 stats at 64px+ between vertical rules on a gradient mesh.
6. *Testimonials*: photo cards with the quote overlaid, or an avatar switcher.
7. *Configurator instead of pricing*: sliders, sticky summary, struck-through price.
8. *FAQ*: 4–12 hairline rows, h2 left.
9. *Closing CTA that repeats the hero pair*, then a footer with a giant cropped wordmark.

**Craft vs AI pages.** Real product UI and real data (names, prices, timestamps). Opacity ladders (1 / 0.6 / 0.4 / 0.2) instead of many grays. Light display weights with negative tracking. Mono for every number and label. One accent, often only inside the product shot. Live states: sold-out pills, struck prices, a document-ready notice, an online-now status. Hairlines and grid lines instead of drop shadows, with shadows reserved for floating UI. Halos blurred past 100px and kept behind objects, never text. Things that bleed off the edges (carousels, wordmarks, cut-outs). Few sections, one idea each. Copy as statements, never "Feature 1/2/3".
